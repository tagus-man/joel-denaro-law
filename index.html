Counsel

Joel M. Denaro

Joel M. Denaro is a Miami-based criminal defense attorney with more than twenty-five years of experience representing clients in state and federal courts throughout South Florida and the country, including the Southern District of New York, the Eastern District of New York, and Washington, D.C.

Born and raised in Miami, he attended Miami Palmetto High School and trained at the Miami-Dade Public Defender's Office for more than six and a half years as an active trial lawyer. During that time, he tried more than 40 jury trials to verdict, ranging from DUI and felony cases to a murder trial in which he secured an acquittal, as well as a death penalty case in which the jury returned a life recommendation. In 2004, he opened his own practice, Joel Denaro, P.A.

His practice spans the full spectrum of criminal defense, including DUI and drug-related charges, serious felony offenses, white-collar fraud, federal investigations, and RICO matters. His work regularly involves money laundering, international money laundering, narcotics offenses, OFAC violations, and Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) matters, often with cross-border exposure spanning Latin America and extradition proceedings.

While in private practice in the Eastern District of New York, he represented the leader of a money laundering organization in what was, at the time, the largest money laundering case in the country, involving $1.8 billion, before Judge Sterling Johnson — the client received no jail sentence. He also represented the leader of a drug trafficking organization in the largest drug case in the Eastern District of New York, involving 880,000 kilograms, in which the client received a sentence of five and a half years. In numerous other large international matters, his clients avoided criminal charges altogether.

Denaro regularly associates with other lawyers and investigators, including former federal law enforcement, and has worked with many of the same professionals throughout his career. His family has also been the subject of extensive South Florida media coverage, reflecting the family's deep roots in Miami's criminal defense bar.

Denaro has been a member of The Florida Bar since 1999 and is a graduate of New York Law School. He has been included in Best Lawyers in America since 2008.

Courtney Lynn Denaro
Family Law Attorney  ·  Joel Denaro, P.A.

Courtney Lynn Denaro earned her undergraduate degree from the University of Vermont before beginning her career at Sotheby's, where she worked as an Art Advisor in Client Services, advising collectors and assisting clients with acquisitions and collection management. She later earned her Juris Doctor from Brooklyn Law School. Ms. Denaro began her legal career as a Staff Attorney for the Civil Court Judges of Florida's Eleventh Judicial Circuit, gaining extensive experience in civil litigation and judicial decision-making. She then practiced family law with Caruana & Lorenzen before joining The Law Offices of Joel Denaro. Drawing on her diverse professional background, Ms. Denaro is known for her thoughtful approach, attention to detail, and commitment to providing practical, client-focused legal representation.
